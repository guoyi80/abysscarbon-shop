FAQ

Common Questions

How long does shipping take?

DHL Express delivers to the US and EU in 5–10 business days. Rest of world: 7–15 business days. Processing takes 1–3 days. Tracking number within 48 hours of shipment.

What if the fins don't fit?

30 days to return any unused item in original packaging. Full refund minus shipping. Custom footpocket sizing is non-refundable unless defective. Measure carefully.

Are these products competition legal?

Yes. C1 Pro blades and N1 noseclip are AIDA / CMAS legal. MF1 monofin meets current CWT regulations. For world-championship level events, email us for compliance verification.

Do you make gear to order? Who makes the gear?

DTC handmade-to-order model. Products are handcrafted in partner workshops in Italy, Japan, and the US. Every unit is individually QC-checked before dispatch. Artisan to you, no warehouse.

What is the Ocean Pledge?

1% of every order is set aside for kelp reforestation, reef restoration and coastal clean-up partners. All orders ship in plastic-free packaging, and every product is built to be serviced and repaired rather than replaced. Numbers are published in our monthly field notes.

I scuba dive, not freedive. Is this gear for me?

Yes. The M1 mask, DC1 computer, N1 noseclip, W1 neck weight and E1 ear plugs are used by recreational and technical scuba divers. Carbon fins are optimized for freediving and spearfishing, but many divers also use them for snorkelling and long surface swims.

Soft, Medium, or Hard flex?

Soft: recreational divers under 70kg. Medium: bestseller, balanced for most. Hard: competitive athletes over 85kg needing max power. Tell us your weight and style — we recommend.

How do I track my order?

DHL tracking link emailed at shipment. Or log into your Snipcart customer account (auto-created at checkout) for live order status.
