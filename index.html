A heuristic, not a guarantee. Weight, working depth, weekly volume, ankle mobility and water type are the drivers — if you are between two stiffnesses, ask for a fitter review before you spend $800 on a blade. Contact us.

Blade Stiffness — weight is one of four inputs

ProfileLoad scoreFlex
Light diver, shallow water, under 2 sessions a weekbelow −0.5Soft
Most recreational and intermediate divers−0.5 to 2.4Medium
Heavy diver, or deep water with 4+ sessions a week2.5 and aboveHard

The calculator above scores seven inputs in one number — weight, depth, weekly volume, discipline, ankle mobility, water type, height. Worked examples at average ankle mobility in salt water:

  • 60kg · 18m · under 2 sessions → −1.0 → Soft
  • 70kg · 20m · 4+ sessions → 1.2 → Medium
  • 76kg · 32m · 2–3 sessions → 1.9 → Medium
  • 64kg · 45m · 4+ sessions → 2.1 → Medium
  • 95kg · 45m · 4+ sessions → 5.2 → Hard

Depth adds 0.75 at 25–39m and 1.5 at 40m+. Weekly volume adds 0.5 at 2–3 sessions and 1.2 at 4+. Competition CWT adds 1, pool dynamic subtracts 1, a stiff ankle subtracts 1.2, very flexible ankles add 0.4, fresh water adds 0.3. Within one grade of a boundary, say so — we hand-check before cutting.

Go one grade softer when

  • You have a stiff ankle or an old injury — a blade you cannot load costs you depth
  • You train fewer than two sessions a week (the blade wins if you overload it)
  • You dive mostly pool dynamic, or you are moving up from fibreglass

We confirm by hand when

  • You land between two stiffness grades — say so at checkout, we review before cutting
  • Your profile is heavy (90kg+) but volume is low, or light with competition volume — these two pull in opposite directions

Stand on paper, heel to wall, and measure to the tip of your longest toe — then add your sock thickness in mm (barefoot 0, 3mm sock +3, 5mm sock +5). Rules that matter:

  • Wide foot or high instep: take the larger band, and tell us — the pocket is fitted by hand
  • Left and right differ: measure both, size to the longer foot
  • Between two bands: take the larger band; a tight pocket cannot be stretched into comfort

FAQ

Common Questions

How long does shipping take?

Shipping is a $29 flat fee per order with DHL Express, and free on orders over $500. Transit is 5–10 business days to the US and EU, 7–15 to the rest of the world. Gear held in stock leaves the workshop in 1–3 business days; made-to-measure suits are cut after you approve your 3D preview, so add production time to the transit estimate. Tracking number within 48 hours of shipment. Duties are separate — see the customs question below.

Will I pay customs duties or import tax?

Possibly, depending on your country. Duties and import taxes are set by your own customs authority and paid by the receiver — they are not included in the product price or the $29 flat shipping fee. DHL notifies you of the amount before delivery. We never declare parcels as gifts to avoid fees. Full rules on our Refunds & Shipping page.

What if the fins don't fit?

30 days to return any unused item in original packaging. Full refund minus shipping. Custom footpocket sizing is non-refundable unless defective. Measure carefully.

Are these products competition legal?

Yes. C1 Pro blades and N1 noseclip are AIDA / CMAS legal. MF1 monofin meets current CWT regulations. For world-championship level events, email us for compliance verification.

Do you make gear to order? Who makes the gear?

DTC handmade-to-order model. Products are handcrafted in partner workshops in Italy, Japan, and the US. Every unit is individually QC-checked before dispatch. Artisan to you, no warehouse.

What is the Ocean Pledge?

1% of every order is set aside for kelp reforestation, reef restoration and coastal clean-up partners. All orders ship in plastic-free packaging, and every product is built to be serviced and repaired rather than replaced. Want the exact figure? Send your order number and we tell you the amount that order set aside and which programme it funded.

I scuba dive, not freedive. Is this gear for me?

Yes. The M1 mask, DC1 computer, N1 noseclip, W1 neck weight and E1 ear plugs are used by recreational and technical scuba divers. Carbon fins are optimized for freediving and spearfishing, but many divers also use them for snorkelling and long surface swims.

Soft, Medium, or Hard flex?

Soft: recreational divers under 70kg. Medium: bestseller, balanced for most. Hard: competitive athletes over 85kg needing max power. Tell us your weight and style — we recommend.

How do I track my order?

DHL tracking link emailed at shipment. Or log into your Snipcart customer account (auto-created at checkout) for live order status.
