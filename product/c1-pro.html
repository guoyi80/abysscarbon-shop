    Build date — layup date, not ship date
    2026-11-13 SAME CARD
    If a pair reaches you more than 30 days after this date, the build sat somewhere. Ask why before accepting delivery — that is a question the card lets you ask.
    Three-point deflection test — what is measured
    Each blade is supported at root and tip and loaded at the mid-point, then repeated at three stations along the blade. Deflection is read in millimetres; the pair is only packed when left and right match inside tolerance.
    Acceptable range and what happens outside it
    Measured at a fixed 2 kg load, 20 °C, blades at rest 24 h after cure:
    • Tip station: 22–28 mm deflection
    • Mid station: 14–18 mm
    • Root station: 6–9 mm
    • Left/right difference: within 8% at every station
    A blade outside the band, or a pair mismatched beyond 8%, is not packed: it is re-laid or scrapped, and your build date moves rather than your standard.

    Remaining questions

    Why $818 and not the list price?

    Launch pricing runs through 30 November 2026, in USD. $895 is the pre-launch list price; on 1 December the item returns to list. The comparison pages on this site quote the same launch prices as the storefront, so the numbers never disagree between pages.

    Is the footpocket included?

    Yes — one footpocket pair, sizes 38–46, fitted to the size you select above. Socks are yours; tell us the thickness because the pocket is sized with them on.

    What happens if the footpocket does not fit?

    Standard sizes can be returned within 30 days unused, in original packaging, refund minus freight. Customised footpocket sizing is non-refundable unless the pocket is defective — which is why the measurement rules on this page are explicit and why we will check a fit by hand before cutting if you ask.

    Do I pay duty?

    Possibly, depending on your country. Duty and import tax are set by your own customs authority, are not included in the product price or in freight, and are paid by the receiver. DHL notifies you of the amount before delivery. Full terms on the Refunds & Shipping page.
