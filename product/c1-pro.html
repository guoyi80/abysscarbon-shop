    Go one grade softer when the ankle is stiff, weekly volume is under two sessions, or you are stepping up from fibreglass. A blade you cannot load costs depth that a stiffer layup never returns.

    We want a hand check when you land between two grades, or your profile pulls in opposite directions (90kg+ with low volume, or sub-65kg with competition volume). Say so in the order notes — the build pauses before the blades are cut.

    Footpocket rules: size from measured foot length plus sock thickness, never from a shoe size. Wide foot or high instep: one band up. Left and right differ: size to the longer foot. Between two bands: take the larger — a tight pocket cannot be stretched into comfort.

    Is the footpocket included?

    Standard sizes can be returned within 30 days unused, in original packaging, refund minus freight. Customised footpocket sizing is non-refundable unless the pocket is defective — which is why the measurement rules on this page are explicit and why we will check a fit by hand before cutting if you ask.

    Possibly, depending on your country. Duty and import tax are set by your own customs authority, are not included in the product price or in freight, and are paid by the receiver. DHL notifies you of the amount before delivery. Full terms on the Refunds & Shipping page.
